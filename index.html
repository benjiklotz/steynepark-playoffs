Tiebreaker rules this tool applies
  1. Division winners take seeds 1–3. Division title: record, then head-to-head, then division record, then Fair Play, then Points For.
  2. Seeds 1–3 (among division winners): record, head-to-head, Fair Play, Points For.
  3. Seeds 4–6 (wild cards, chosen one at a time): record, head-to-head, Fair Play, Points For. Positions 7–12 are ranked the same way.
  4. Head-to-head only settles a tie if one team has played and beaten every other tied team in every meeting (it takes the seed), or has played and lost to every other tied team in every meeting (it is eliminated and the tiebreak restarts from the top). A 1-1 split or an unplayed matchup does neither.
  5. Division record keeps only the team(s) with the best record among those still tied; everyone else is eliminated and the tiebreak restarts. Fair Play eliminates the lowest team(s) remaining, then restarts. If all remaining teams are level, the next tiebreaker applies.
  6. Points For is the last resort: highest total takes the seed.
